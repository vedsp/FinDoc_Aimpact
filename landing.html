Core Features

Everything You Need for Financial Compliance

From secure document uploads to AI-powered classification and risk scoring — FinDocAI gives auditors and analysts superpowers.

Secure Document Upload

Upload PDF, PNG, JPG, CSV, XLS/XLSX files (up to 25 MB each). All files are stored securely per user with JWT authentication.

AI-Powered Classification

Automatically classifies documents as Bank Statements, GST Returns, ITR, Invoices, Balance Sheets, Salary Slips, Loan Documents, and more.

Live Dashboard Analytics

Real-time overview of document counts, bank transaction credits/debits, net cash flow, anomaly count, and risk score — all in one place.

Anomaly Detection

Identify suspicious transactions and irregularities in financial data automatically with built-in anomaly flagging and risk assessment.

PDF Text Extraction

Powered by PyMuPDF — instantly extracts text content from uploaded PDFs for fast review and record indexing without manual data entry.

Report Export

Generate and export financial reports as PDF directly in the browser using jsPDF — share summaries with your team instantly.
